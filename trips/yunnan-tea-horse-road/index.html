Yunnan Tea Horse Road

A private nine-day Yunnan route connecting Dali, Lijiang, Tiger Leaping Gorge and Shangri-La through Naxi culture, mountain scenery and historic tea routes.

Plan with Morrow Travel · Explore Journeys

9-day Southwest China itinerary

Travel style: Culture & Slow Travel. Best for: Culture lovers, couples and slow travelers. Suggested travel time: March to June and September to November.

Journey highlights

  • Dali Ancient Town
  • Lijiang Old Town
  • Tiger Leaping Gorge
  • Shangri-La monasteries

Sample route

  1. Arrive in Dali
  2. Erhai villages and old town
  3. Lijiang and Naxi culture
  4. Tiger Leaping Gorge
  5. Shangri-La monasteries
  6. Plateau village day
  7. Departure or Kunming extension

This itinerary is a starting point rather than a fixed package. Morrow Travel can adapt pacing, hotels, guide support and regional connections to your group and dates.
