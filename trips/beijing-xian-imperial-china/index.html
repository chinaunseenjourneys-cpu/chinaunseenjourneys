Beijing & Xi'an Imperial China

A private seven-day introduction to China linking Beijing's Great Wall, Forbidden City and hutongs with Xi'an's Terracotta Warriors and old city.

Plan with Morrow Travel · Explore Journeys

7-day Imperial China itinerary

Travel style: Culture & History. Best for: First-time visitors, families and senior travelers. Suggested travel time: April to June and September to October.

Journey highlights

  • Mutianyu Great Wall
  • Forbidden City
  • Hutong visit
  • Terracotta Warriors

Sample route

  1. Arrive in Beijing
  2. Forbidden City and hutongs
  3. Great Wall day
  4. Temple of Heaven and train to Xi'an
  5. Terracotta Warriors
  6. Xi'an old city
  7. Departure

This itinerary is a starting point rather than a fixed package. Morrow Travel can adapt pacing, hotels, guide support and regional connections to your group and dates.
