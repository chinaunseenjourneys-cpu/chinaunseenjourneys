Explore private China itineraries

Zhangjiajie Avatar Mountains

5 days · Central China · Nature & Landscapes

A private five-day Zhangjiajie journey through sandstone pillars, Yuanjiajie, Tianzi Mountain and Tianmen Mountain, paced around weather, walking comfort and crowd patterns.

Best for: Photographers, couples and families with older children. Suggested seasons: March to May and September to November.

Beijing & Xi'an Imperial China

7 days · Imperial China · Culture & History

A private seven-day introduction to China linking Beijing's Great Wall, Forbidden City and hutongs with Xi'an's Terracotta Warriors and old city.

Best for: First-time visitors, families and senior travelers. Suggested seasons: April to June and September to October.

Yunnan Tea Horse Road

9 days · Southwest China · Culture & Slow Travel

A private nine-day Yunnan route connecting Dali, Lijiang, Tiger Leaping Gorge and Shangri-La through Naxi culture, mountain scenery and historic tea routes.

Best for: Culture lovers, couples and slow travelers. Suggested seasons: March to June and September to November.

Tibet Private Plateau Journey

8 days · Himalayan Plateau · Spiritual Culture

A private eight-day Tibet journey based in Lhasa with monastery visits, permit coordination, acclimatization time and flexible high-altitude routing.

Best for: Experienced travelers interested in spiritual culture and landscapes. Suggested seasons: May to October.

Guilin & Yangshuo Family Escape

6 days · South China · Family Journeys

A relaxed six-day family journey through Guilin and Yangshuo with karst scenery, the Li River, countryside cycling, bamboo rafting and child-friendly pacing.

Best for: Families, multi-generation groups and soft-adventure travelers. Suggested seasons: April to October.

Luxury Shanghai, Suzhou & Hangzhou

6 days · East China · Luxury Escapes

A polished six-day private East China itinerary combining Shanghai design and dining, Suzhou gardens, Hangzhou's West Lake and Longjing tea country.

Best for: Couples, luxury travelers and design or food lovers. Suggested seasons: March to June and September to December.
